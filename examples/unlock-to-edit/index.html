Your draft and its limits

Both settings are saved together on committed edits, restoration, and re-locking. Each write is immediately read back and compared. There is no server save, live service or durable backup.

Changing sections or appearance preserves open edits and restoration points. Reload restores successfully stored settings, starts both fields locked, and discards restoration points. Appearance and text size reset on reload.

Restore means the value at the start of that field’s editing session, even after autosave. It is one restoration point, not history. Loading example defaults intentionally replaces both fields, including locked values, and re-locks them only after a verified save.

Other tabs can overwrite this browser-local draft; conflict handling and cross-device synchronization are not provided. A lock protects direct editing, not permissions or security.
